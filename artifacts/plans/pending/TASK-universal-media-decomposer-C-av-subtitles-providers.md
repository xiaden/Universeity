# Task: Universal Media Decomposer — Audio, Video, Subtitles, Providers, and Sandbox

## Problem Statement

Add the remaining required v1 modality baselines and model/provider boundary without weakening the untrusted-media security posture. Audio, video, and every subtitle track must remain source-native evidence with exact timing and provenance. Model output is structured, swappable, confidence-scoped, and never an opaque semantic authority. This plan depends on Plans A–B for event, locator, stage, OCFL, and sandbox contracts.

**Prerequisite:** `TASK-universal-media-decomposer-B-jobs-text-image.md`. Use `artifacts/designs/parts/universal-media-decomposer/CONTRACTS.md`.

## Completion Criteria

- All audio/video/subtitle subprocesses execute behind the documented sandbox boundary; raw source and derived artifacts remain recoverable after parser failure.
- Audio emits timing/ASR/music/SFX/language/speaker candidates with hallucination filtering and promotion ban; diarization licensing/access is explicitly gated and fallback works.
- Video emits PTS-native segments and composes audio plus independent subtitle sources; timestamp-map normalization and encoding preservation are proven by fixtures.
- Provider substitution, security, modality integration, tolerance/determinism, static/type/lint, and build/license/CVE gates pass or are explicitly blocked with named gate evidence.
